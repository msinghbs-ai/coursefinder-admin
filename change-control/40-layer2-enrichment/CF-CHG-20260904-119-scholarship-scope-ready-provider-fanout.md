# CF-CHG-20260904-119 — Scholarship Scope Ready-Provider Fan-out

**Status:** IMPLEMENTED / TARGETED PASS  
**Milestone:** M2.4.5

State/country Scholarship firing was hardened to avoid creating jobs for every geographically matched Provider. The preview reports total scope coverage separately from acquisition-ready coverage, and only ready Providers are fanned out.

This is a cost/operability safeguard. Missing Providers remain explicit qualification gaps rather than false job activity.
