Destination data › Study rules. Every visa, work and post-study rule is an effective-dated version. The capture step proposes a typed value with the quote it came from; a person approves and schedules it. Nothing becomes current on its own.

One mechanism for every row. The route in step 2 depends on the kind of source; everything after it is shared.

Step 1Register sourceLayer 1 › Sources: URL, owner, rights mode, cadence, page section. Platform Admin approves.
Step 2FetchRegister file (L1) · statistical edition (Dataset ETL) · policy page (page capture; Firecrawl only if direct fetch fails).
Step 3ProposeParser for files. For pages, a pinned OpenRouter model proposes the typed value and the exact quote. Unchanged page hash skips this.
Step 4ValidateUnits, currency, dates, overlap with current version, count change. Failures held with a plain reason.
Step 5Review in Layer 4A person approves and schedules, edits, or rejects. Activation is its own step.
Step 6PublishNew publication generation; Website v3 destination action reads it; Data Delivery shows coverage.

What the Wix site calls and how it lands on the page. Catalogue sections use the existing v3 actions; the new destination action serves study rules, careers and cities, paged and pinned to a publication generation.
