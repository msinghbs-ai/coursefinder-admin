## A. Consumer API update (for the website developer)

> **Subject:** CourseFinder API: what's new (11 Oct)
>
> Hi,
>
> Here's today's update on CourseFinder data available through the API. There are no contract or field changes. Two quality changes affect the numbers below, and both are intended.
>
> **1. Only the provider's own website (or the government register) is now used as a source.** Course pages and course details that came from third-party course directories have been removed. That's why the number of official course page links went down slightly today. The remaining links all point to the university's or college's own site.
