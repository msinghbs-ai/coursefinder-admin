data team
Scope: Universities, courses and scholarships for the Study Australia destination page in Wix.
Data snapshot: 2 November 2026, India time.
Purpose: Describe the data received, its use in Wix, coverage gaps and the data needed to support reliable
discovery and detailed information.


1. Evidence and catalogue scope
The live audit covered every Australia provider-list, course-search and scholarship-list page, all 45 university
detail responses, 26 selected course details and 8 selected scholarship details. The list totals matched unique
IDs, with no non-Australian records in those lists. Course and scholarship detail findings are samples unless
explicitly stated otherwise.

These figures describe API responses. They do not establish independent verification against university
websites or the contents of the published Wix CMS. “Missing” means absent, null or empty in the audited field; it
does not establish that the underlying requirement or opportunity does not exist.

Provider classifications are 728 private VET colleges, 265 schools, 137 higher education institutes, 80 other
providers, 45 universities, 16 TAFE/polytechnics and 8 university colleges. These are API classifications; counts

total are different measures.

The Australia reference response contains 20 study levels, 79 study areas and 521 campus-city entries.
Provider, course and scholarship IDs are the joining keys; names are display labels.


2. How Wix consumes the data
The destination page is a native Wix Custom Element. It requests data from the site's own backend, which
reads Wix CMS records or makes authenticated StudySearch Website API v2 requests. The upstream API
credential stays in Wix Secrets Manager.

Flow: Australia page → Wix /_functions/studysearch → Wix backend → Wix CMS or StudySearch API →
page data and detail views.

University and scholarship lists are loaded for local filtering and sorting. Course searches send filters to the API,
including keyword, provider ID, study area/level, campus city, state, intake month, institution type, delivery
mode, English-test code, annual tuition ceiling and scholarship availability.

Course searches use 50 records per API request. The university detail course preview loads up to 50 courses;
its intake counts describe that preview, not the entire university catalogue. The individual course detail request
supplies fields that may be absent from a search result. Scholarship detail currently uses the loaded scholarship
record; the API also offers a richer individual scholarship response.

The configured CMS refresh cadence is six hours for delta updates and 24 hours for reference data. Live
responses have a 15-minute backend cache; list/reference data may also remain in browser memory for the
current page session. These are consumption settings, not promises about source-verification frequency.
Source-update, projection-update and last-verified dates must remain distinct.

The audited provider, scholarship and course-search list actions accept page sizes of 1-50. Consumers need
the total, actual page size and all pages to establish complete coverage. Detail-only fields should not be
classified as missing because they are absent from a list response.

Within the 11,744 university-course subset, 5,097 lack intake months, 227 lack any tuition amount, 931 lack
annual tuition, 5,848 lack a fee year, 2,002 lack English requirement records, 5,218 lack minimum band scores
and 2,920 lack an official course URL.

The search entry summary is English-only for 16,044 courses and has no basis for 7,688. The separate
university entry matrices remain relevant; absence of academic summaries in search results does not imply
absence of every form of academic data.

Fee and eligibility interpretation
12,202 courses flag annual tuition as derived. Recorded fee bases are 7,896 annual, 15,018 course total, 460
indicative annual and 358 missing. A raw course-total amount must retain that basis; it must not become an
annual fee without a supported calculation. Preserve currency, year, source and annual_is_derived alongside
the amount. Clarify whether additional compulsory charges are included and which intake/student cohort a fee
applies to.

English requirements need their source scope. A university-policy threshold is different from a requirement
verified for a specific course. Overall and minimum-band requirements are separate fields. An English score
does not establish academic eligibility.

8,917 courses have a positive scholarship count. Associated provider awards do not, by themselves, confirm
course, nationality or study-level eligibility. Funding records should remain separate from tuition until award
applicability and value are confirmed.

Full-course detail sample and data needed
All 26 sampled full course records lack descriptions. All have fee history; 21 contain scholarship records and 24
contain provider outcomes. Twenty have English-only entry summaries and six have no entry summary. All 26
have empty/null structured minimum scores, prerequisites, selection tasks and requirements notes in entry. This
is sample evidence, not a census of every course detail.

Benefit classifications: 664 fixed cash, 144 percentage of tuition, 50 full tuition and 34 unclassified. All 664

Data-quality gaps requiring clarification
Eligibility scope. An empty nationality list is not confirmation of worldwide eligibility. Null application, renewal or
study-load flags are unknown, not false. All 755 populated study-level lists identify linked courses as their
source; these derived levels are not independent award rules. Preserve study_levels_source and distinguish
verified eligibility from inferred associations.

Benefit completeness. Notes can contain additional cash, accommodation, first-year-only conditions, recurring
payments or ranges. Preserve them together with structured values and tiers. For Alex Chernov Scholarship, the
response describes full tuition plus up to $20,000; a full-tuition classification alone does not describe the whole
benefit. Some notes are noisy extracted fragments and need editorial/source validation before becoming
student-facing prose.

Application status versus dates. Three records are marked open with a 1 November 2026 deadline in the 2
November snapshot:

      Scott Reid Memorial Scholarship - Griffith University.
      The University of Sydney Law School Dean's Scholarship.
      Computing and Information Systems PhD Scholarship - University of Melbourne.

Reconcile these records and define the date/timezone and refresh rules for status transitions. Distinguish rolling
deadlines, multiple rounds, unknown dates and genuinely closed awards.

6. Data contract and handoff priorities
The following are requested data improvements and clarifications, not a confirmed delivery roadmap.

  1. Academic criteria: Expand provider matrix coverage and populate course-specific academic fields.
     Establish explicit links between country/qualification thresholds, benchmark bands, conditions and
     applicable courses.
  2. Intakes: Supply intake year, exact start date and application deadline where known, with timezone and
     rolling/unknown-date semantics.
  3. Scholarship eligibility and benefits: Resolve the 34 unclassified awards, preserve mixed benefits and
     payment periods, improve nationality/application/academic coverage, and reconcile status/date conflicts.
  4. Fees and provenance: Fill missing years/amounts/official URLs; preserve source, currency, total-versus-
     annual basis, derived flags and effective cohort/intake.
  5. Descriptions and outcomes: Supply verified university/course descriptions and distinguish unavailable
     surveys from admitted-but-empty outcome records. Retain provider grain, study level, survey, reference
     year, unit and available statistical context.
  6. Associations and freshness: Clarify scholarship-course association rules and truncation retrieval;
     preserve stable IDs, update/removal semantics, source timestamps and verification dates across sync and
     detail responses.

For Wix consumption, each field should have a documented meaning, stable type, allowed values, null
semantics, provenance and applicability. Keep “unknown,” “not applicable,” “not collected” and confirmed
negative values distinct. Current summary counts and historical records should remain separate. Rank bands
must retain their published display values and edition years; provider-wide outcomes must not be represented
as course outcomes or individual predictions.

Data already exposed includes provider entry matrices, structured course-entry objects and richer scholarship
detail fields. Their presence in the schema is separate from population coverage. Any upcoming expansion
should identify which records, fields and countries/qualifications will be populated and provide an expected
delivery date confirmed by the data team.
