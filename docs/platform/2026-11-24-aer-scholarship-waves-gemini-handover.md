## 6. Lessons learnt
1. Combined discovery and extraction stalled AU; staged ownership lets source-ready evidence feed readers continuously.
2. 45-provider waves, planning-only replies, queue waiting and repeated diagnosis waste human engagement/agent limits. Long execution sessions, short reports; independent progress while other work settles.
3. A registered URL, successful parse, proposed rows (including misleading run rows_admitted), admitted rows and consumer-published values are distinct. Use actual canonical and ledger counts, distinct provider/course/award IDs; distinguish gross and net change, per-country and cohort denominators.
4. Repair shared reader structure rather than provider-specific hacks; reuse stored evidence, paid answers, qualified deterministic v2 path. No invented grades, country, levels or scholarship scope. Preserve strict proof and genuine Layer 4/manual locks.
5. Source evidence must be usable, official, exact identity, edition/campus/qualification/level. Missing/ambiguous source is a source problem, not an admission defect.
6. Pipeline effective state, not old cron active flag. Successful ticks are not result-bearing cycles. Shared controls/qualification/spend only through authorised owner.
7. Scope on-demand request to provider/purpose/time and cap; estimate and credit balance before, actual/remaining and close after settlement. Account reserve still applies; cannot move scheduled work onto requests to escape limits. Record failed calls; don't conflate account delta and session expenditure.
8. Central source of truth is Admin; M3 is a mirror. No duplicate tracker, no stale Sheet presented as current. Exact PR, branch/head, request and next executable action enable context rollover. S3 chats fill up: handovers use pointers, not copied transcripts.
9. Fee Feedback-4 investigation #978 is with S1-Build and frontend; keep independent AER/Scholarship moving, avoid conflicting shared migrations or stopping fee fix.
