## Method and limits

- **Windows:** 24 hours and 7 days. Cron history is retained from 2026-11-22T16:48 UTC, so cron 7-day figures are partial; dispatcher run logs cover the full 7 days.
- **Outcome classes (from each run's own recorded status and result):**
  - productive: an admission, publication or hand-off counter above 0;
  - held: only protective counters above 0;
  - empty: a structured result with every counter at 0;
  - failed or deferred: from the recorded status;
  - unmeasured: no structured result.
- **Daily load:** runs a day (from the schedule period) × average seconds a run. It is the database time the cadence costs.
- **Not attributed:** toolset spend (AI and Firecrawl) is not split per component, because no register links a job to a service. It is reported by service in `pim.operations.toolset`.
- **Dependencies:** upstream is the dispatcher a lane, rule or target rides on. Downstream is the field or fill function in its register row. A cron job's own dependencies are not registered.
