## 3. How a reviewer reaches these

1. Layer 4 Review › **Inbox** (the only Layer 4 tab; Platform Admin signed in).
2. Switch to the **University** view and pick the university; choose the kind pill **Entry requirements (AER)**.
3. The AER summary line shows pending / ready to decide / technically held counts for that university. Keep the **Ready to decide** toggle on (the default) to see only decidable items, oldest first, 25 per page with Previous / Next; **All waiting** shows the rest.
4. Each card loads its packet when it scrolls into view. Open "Open the stored copy" to see the page itself.
5. Decide on the card: **Approve all rows**, **Approve except…** (tick the rows to leave out) or **Reject the read**, each with one reason of at least 5 characters. Decisions are one item at a time; AER items are excluded from the generic mass-decision path by an earlier Platform Admin decision (30 Oct 2026 A2/A3). Decided items appear under Inbox › Decided.
6. Note for the reader: the Inbox "Ready to decide" figure is calculated from a different stored classification (settlement) than the 184 here. See section 6.

## 8. Method and limits

- Read only. Queries on the live project covered the review items, readiness table, proposed rows, stored-copy records, locks, settlement classes and the live gate. A mistaken attempt at a temporary view failed and changed nothing.
- "Number found in quote" compares a stored score with the numbers in its own quote; it does not read the stored page itself. Whether a quote is truly on the page is the gate's own test and is not repeated here.
- Quote length 300 is inferred from 57 stored quotes of exactly that length; the cap's source was not traced.
- No AER ownership claim exists for any university today. A claim is made with `security.layer4_aer_owner_claim_v1`, which this task did not use.
- Full item ids are in `pipeline.layer4_review_items`; the 8-character Ref is the card's own identifier and is unique across the 184.
