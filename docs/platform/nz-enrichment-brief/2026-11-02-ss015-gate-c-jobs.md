`pipeline.automation_catalogue` has no criticality field. Its columns are jobname, area, sort, label, description, control_rank, batch_editable and layer23_stage. "Critical" is therefore **my classification**. I based it on two things:
- the health-check rules that page a person (OPERATIONAL-LIMITS, Monitoring › Critical);

- (a) stale or break what the website or Zoho reads;
