## Root cause

Job `exit-awards-apply` (:37 hourly) calls `security.exit_awards_apply_v2`, which sends the award page address with its `#/overview?studentType=int` fragment. In `security.coverage_apply_course_v1`, `on conflict (course_id, link_type, url) do update set status = 'active'` then re-activated an inactive link at that address.

## The fix covers every route

A trigger on `catalogue.course_links` covers every writer of the table, including those that exist only in the live database:

- superseded links;
- new spellings of a superseded page;
- the Layer 4 "take" rule;
- held links (remediation hold or pending review);
- rejected links;
- retired links.

Manual locks still apply first. A person or a signed-in Layer 4 reviewer can still restore a link on purpose.
