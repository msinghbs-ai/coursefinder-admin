## Approved policy design — zero item-by-item approvals for safe descriptions
Implement an explicit configurable auto-admit route **only** for a defined safe class:
1. Verified provider identity: canonical provider ID and registered name, country, official website/source or authoritative official register; no cross-brand/third-party page, no ambiguous college vs university linkage; preserve manual locks and previous human rejections.
2. Every factual sentence grounded in a named official source and extract/registry fields, with evidence identifier, source URL or registry edition, captured timestamp, normalised quote/fact, source hash, model/parser version and exact provider ID. Strict host/page identity, claim span, content freshness and year checks.
3. Prohibit inferred rankings, excellence, accreditation, sponsorship, scholarships, student outcomes, course availability, campus locations, visa eligibility, finances or promotional superlatives unless separately verified under their own existing governed sources and explicit rule. Prefer short useful neutral descriptions, not generic brand filler.
4. **Tier 1:** reuse existing approved stored official About / provider pages, extract meaningful verified factual sentences; no new fetch charge.
5. **Tier 2:** direct free read of official provider site/About pages; check aliases and identity before quote verification.
6. **Tier 3:** budgeted Firecrawl map/scrape only for otherwise eligible providers not resolved by tiers 1–2, dedupe by host/content hash and honour paid-credit controls; do not crawl whole sites indiscriminately.
7. **Tier 4 (no About page):** only if credible registered-provider facts are available, deterministically construct a **minimal factual profile** from the verified register/provider fields and, where verified, active catalogue offerings (identity, type, location and what it teaches). Clearly tag `registry_factual` provenance, with no pretence it came from an About page and no fabricated statements. If facts are too thin even for a useful, truthful profile, leave an explicit `insufficient_verified_facts` exception rather than a fake description. New tier must pass an **explicitly implemented admission authority** and source-parity tests before write.
8. For Tier 1–4 source-verified claims meeting objective rules, **automatic admission is approved as a *policy change***, but the technical mechanism must be a specifically scoped audited path, not a batch invocation of a human approval procedure or direct mass UPDATE. Preserve existing Layer 4 items and account for them through recorded auto-settlement with distinct machine actor/rule; never mislabel as human decisions. Invalid/promotional/conflicting/identity/locked cases continue to hold with exact reason and next source/revisit, not silently discarded.
9. Per-provider idempotence, evidence/hash change triggers recheck, provenance, before/after audit, rollback/withdrawal path, consumer source refresh, Website/Zoho parity and same-provider conflict protection. Consumer APIs stay G3 frozen.
