## 2. New objective: system autonomy, not “30,302 fabricated exact dates”
**Acceptance of an autonomous production intake feature today** means:
1. Existing source discovery and evidence readers automatically select eligible providers/courses in all supported countries, not just a top-ten pilot, and move past unsupported/held entities with a recorded next action.

5. **At least two real unattended :26 production cycles after release**, with expected vs actual eligible, reviewed/held, newly admitted and published values, source/consumer evidence; target measurable **positive retained net gain from more than one provider** if any verified eligible candidate exists. If none qualify, prove the next provider/source route actually ran and classify the gate honestly; do not claim delivery acceptance.
6. Every active, eligible undated row eventually cycles through the source/evidence ladder or receives a truthful `month_only_valid` / `no_verified_exact_day` / identity / lifecycle / human-hold status; no fixed 20-university end.

**Not a valid KPI:** count of queued jobs, PRs, green cron executions, proposed calendars or filled-event gross totals without before/after canonical and consumer parity.

## 5. Timing maths — why “all dates today” is not a defensible ETA
Current cron is one **:26 hourly** apply with a **200-row** configured/default batch. At that measured limit, simply considering 30,302 active undated rows once requires **ceil(30,302/200)=152 hourly cycles**, **at least 152 scheduled hours (6.3 days)**; this even assumes every row is eligible, zero retries, and no policy/source work. Revisiting 8,793 once under the same single 200-row-per-hour process requires **44 hours** minimum if all slots served them; #931 deliberately distributes them over **14 days**. Therefore “all 30,302 exact dates by 23:59” is *both throughput-impossible at current cadence and factually invalid for month-only courses*.

The only defensible same-day deliverable is **an operationally accepted autonomous process which produces valid net gain and keeps processing all eligible work**, subject to lawful release and actual evidence. After a genuine release and at least two unattended cycles, calculate the remaining evidence-eligible backlog and ETA using observed retained correct publications/hour (and separate source acquisition and genuinely month-only exceptions). If the rate is zero, ETA is **UNKNOWN / BLOCKED**, not infinity or a made-up date.
