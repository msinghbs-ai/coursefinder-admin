**11 November 2026 AEDT · Proposed for planning · Estimates in AUD excluding GST**

StudySearch supplies governed destination data and its developer handoff. Wix owns page presentation and static editorial content. Extend the current Supabase and Cloudflare platform with country adapters and shared structured-data modules. Preserve the supplied release order and RM numbering, including the gaps.

For the late-December 2026 Study Square handoff, scope the first delivery to verified Australian fields available through the current Website contract. An illustrative 40–80 engineering hours within RM1 covers a bounded field and publication reconciliation, sample payloads, gaps and developer handoff for already-published data. ## Workbook reconciliation

Reviewed all **34 worksheets**, including 28 destination tabs, Country overview, Read me, Template map, Still to confirm, Source check and Business schools. The 28 destination sheets contain **886 source items**. The 20 requested destinations account for **677**. Template map has 20 comparable dataset rows. Source check contains **974 hyperlink mentions across 677 distinct targets**, including 56 commercial-publisher rows. The workbook lists **69 open items**, eight rated High, including four in the requested rollout (Sweden dependants, Spain funds, Malta conflicting work hours, Netherlands dependants). This is a design and workbook audit. Link reachability, legal terms and current policy for every source require country-level verification.

Workbook status totals are 518 To load, 256 To add, 44 Not available, 37 In prototype Supabase and 31 Loaded. These are worksheet labels. Before release reconcile the original source, ingest rights, canonical grain, existing data and authorised consumer output. The Read me says degree-level/full-time/on-campus, while AU/CA/NZ tabs and overview counts include other award levels. Adopt the narrower initial Study Square slice and explicitly scope local exceptions. Confirm full-time study load and physical attendance independently. Keep local language, English tests, immigration tests and course admission tests distinct. For outcomes, explicitly label general or unknown cohorts, and exclude domestic-only results from international comparisons. Never convert no observation to zero.

Reviewed outside this rollout and left unsequenced: Poland, Italy, Greece, Cyprus, Thailand, Malaysia, South Korea, Japan.

Use shared dimensions for country, subdivision, provider, campus, course, credential, currency and time. Business school accreditations need entity, issuer, accreditation type, scope, dates and evidence. Award triple accreditation only when all three grants are individually proven at the applicable grain. National quality results preserve original scheme and labels. Outcome metrics preserve survey, year, cohort, study field, sample and method. Keep comparable metrics subject to explicit mappings.

Visa, work and minimum wage belong in Supabase as effective-dated facts with authority, source, review, expiry and explicit conditions. Model processing times as dated statistics with origin/route scope. Model city costs and climate as separately sourced observations with locality and period. Link student cost baskets to the applicable population, cadence and currency. Visa financial thresholds are policy facts with their own effective dates. Publish official source links with material facts. Keep current, future and withdrawn versions, and hold conflicting or expired claims.

Use existing official-source and evidence machinery for read, validation, replay, review, admission and publication. Keep private object paths portable, deduplicate by content hash, retain the latest usable snapshot and protected audit evidence, and apply the approved retention policy. Prefer official bulk feeds/APIs and direct reads. Use governed Firecrawl for discovery/rendering, Parse for proven adapter gaps, and OpenRouter only where deterministic extraction is insufficient and quote-backed verification applies. Measure credits, model input/output tokens and elapsed time per successful admission. Reuse paid answers on retries.

### Supabase capacity and recurring costs

Live on 11 November 2026 at 21:22 AEDT: **5.42 GB logical Postgres**, **30.69 GB object storage** and 230,939 objects. Storage split is 22.75 GB evidence, 7.71 GB adapter captures and 0.22 GB archives and provider assets combined. Latest recorded compute was **Medium / 4 GB RAM**. Physical disk allocation, organisation-wide egress, actual billing and external licence charges were not measured. Existing evidence-only planning thresholds are 60/75/90% of 60 GiB. Track that policy separately from all-bucket totals and billed storage.

Base sizing uses 184,500 incremental course rows at 50 kB indexed per course, 579,000 other facts at 2 kB each and 187,500 distinct URL candidates × 80% captured × 1.5 retained versions. Add 7 kB indexed metadata and 220 kB compressed object per retained version. This gives **17.38 GB logical DB** and **80.19 GB total objects** at full rollout. Apply 30% headroom, round physical disk up to a planning increment and budget **25 GB disk / 105 GB object envelope**. At twice the incremental volume, plan roughly **40 GB disk / 170 GB objects** with headroom. Both forecasts are proxies. Re-estimate from actual persisted row widths, WAL/temp usage, evidence size, retention, and dedupe after the first cohorts.

Keep the existing project and Medium compute for the launch scenario. Check physical allocation and Supabase spend controls before staged imports. Review disk, bucket and egress trends, and CPU, query latency, active connections, queue lag and cron overlap under measured load before scaling compute. Refresh source data incrementally and reuse existing search and Worker paths. Separate backup/restore validation for Postgres and evidence objects. Price any PITR, replicas or additional recovery services separately.
