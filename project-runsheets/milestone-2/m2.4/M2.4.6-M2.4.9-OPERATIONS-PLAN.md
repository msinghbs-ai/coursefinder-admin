# M2.4.6–M2.4.9 Operations Plan

**Status:** PROPOSED PROGRAMME SEQUENCE / PRE-M2.5 OPERATIONS READINESS  
**Effective:** 15 September 2026  
**Authority:** `PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, `docs/README.md`, M2 Standing Instructions/Addenda and applicable Change Controls.

## Purpose

Insert four bounded operational sub-milestones between M2.4.5 and M2.5 so Production begins from a proven operating model rather than continuing design and tuning inside M2.5.

Programme sequence:

`M2.4.5 close/freeze → M2.4.6 Operate → M2.4.7 Scale → M2.4.8 Consume/Support → M2.4.9 Rehearse → M2.5 Production`

## M2.4.6 — Production Operations Model

Define and prove the day-to-day operating model: dispatcher/waves, Evidence reuse first, deterministic L2, bounded L3, L4 escalation, retries/parking, stale recovery, idempotency, quotas/cost ceilings, stop conditions, operator ownership and operational metrics.

## M2.4.7 — Controlled Operational Scale

Exercise the operating model against realistic AU/NZ provider/state/country scopes with qualified sources only. Measure throughput, enrichment gain, failures, cost, latency and recovery behaviour. Do not bypass source qualification or canonical authority controls to increase coverage.

## M2.4.8 — Consumer & Data Operations Readiness

Make accepted enrichment operationally usable by governed consumers and support teams: Search/publication reconciliation, Wix/API contract readiness, coverage/freshness reporting, operational dashboards, support/runbook procedures and operator-visible backlog/health signals.

## M2.4.9 — Production Dress Rehearsal & Go/No-Go

Run the final non-Production rehearsal: migration manifest, environment/secrets manifest, backup/restore and rollback rehearsal, scheduler/wave rehearsal, capacity/quota checks, security review, full nominated UAT and explicit Production-entry GO/NO-GO.

## M2.5 entry condition

M2.5 remains the Production establishment and controlled-launch milestone. It should start only after M2.4.9 records an explicit GO. M2.5 is not the place to discover or design routine dispatcher, retry, enrichment-authority, cost-control or operator procedures.

## Branching convention

Use milestone-scoped integration branches from the accepted predecessor baseline:

- `m2-4-6-production-operations`
- `m2-4-7-operational-scale`
- `m2-4-8-consumer-ops-readiness`
- `m2-4-9-production-dress-rehearsal`

Feature/fix branches may run underneath each integration branch. Do not keep a single catch-all pre-production branch open across all four sub-milestones.

## M2.4.5 handoff rule

Finish only the outstanding M2.4.5/CF-245 reconciliation and acceptance work under M2.4.5. Accepted CF-245 outputs become the baseline for M2.4.6. New operational design, scaling and production-wave tuning belong in M2.4.6 onward.
